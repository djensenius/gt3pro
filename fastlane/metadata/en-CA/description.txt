Scooter Companion — your GT3 Pro ride tracker.

Connect to your GT3 Pro over Bluetooth, see live speed, battery, and range on your lock screen, and automatically log every ride with GPS routes, heart rate, and detailed telemetry.

LIVE DASHBOARD
• Real-time speed, battery, range, and gear mode
• Live Activity on your lock screen and Dynamic Island
• Standby mode shows when your scooter is connected but powered off

AUTOMATIC RIDE LOGGING
• Rides start and stop automatically based on speed
• GPS route recording with map visualization
• Surface roughness tracking via motion sensors
• Heart rate recording via Apple Watch companion

APPLE WATCH
• See speed, battery, and distance on your wrist
• HealthKit workout tracking with heart rate
• Auto-starts when you start riding

DETAILED TELEMETRY
• Battery voltage, current, temperature, and state of charge
• Body temperature monitoring
• Gear mode tracking (Walk, Eco, Sport, Race)
• Trip distance and time
• Estimated range

RIDE HISTORY
• Browse past rides with distance, speed, and battery stats
• Cloud sync with your personal FluxHaus server
• Grafana dashboard integration for deep analytics

SCOOTER INFO
• Odometer, firmware versions, serial number
• Battery health diagnostics
• Real-time error and warning codes

Requires a SuperScooter GT3 Pro and a FluxHaus server account for cloud sync. Built with privacy in mind — all data stays on your device unless you choose to sync.
